The Cord Suit is a digital archive for the [Silver Jews][1], an American
band formed in 1989 by [David Berman][2] along with [Stephen Malkmus][3]
and [Bob Nastanovich][4].

This site started life in 1997 as *The Corduroy Suit*. It collected
articles, photographs, guitar tabs, drawings and other ephemera from
fans around the world. David Berman occasionally contributed memorabilia
and kind words. The archive lives on here.

Many thanks to the following people for their contributions:
Jason Benjamin, David Berman, Vassilis Bibas, Travis Bursik, Kody Ford,
Peter Jenks, Adalena Kavanagh, Stephen McRobbie, Katrina Mitchell,
Alex Parish, Gate Pratt, Omri Rosen, Justin Taylor, David Todd,
Ryan Walsh and David Weintraub.
